AI Shuffle is the phone companion for AI Shuffle, the self-hosted agent workspace from HobbyCoders. Your agents run on your own AI Shuffle worker: a home server, an Unraid box, a Docker host or the AI Shuffle desktop app. This app connects to it so you can keep working away from your desk.

What you can do
• Start sessions and chat with your agents, with streaming replies and file downloads.
• Approve or deny tool requests from anywhere, so long-running work does not stall.
• Talk to an agent hands-free with voice chat.
• Switch between several workers, on your home network or through protected remote access.
• Optionally let an agent use phone tools you allow, such as timers or maps, with every action approved by you.

Your worker, your accounts
AI Shuffle does not provide AI models. Agents run on your worker with the AI accounts and API keys you set up there. Conversations and files stay on your worker; the optional AI Shuffle account only links your devices and workers.

Requirements
• A running AI Shuffle worker (sold separately). This app does not run agents on the phone.
• Android 8.0 or newer.

Try the offline demo from Settings to see the app without a worker or an account.

Delete your account at any time from Settings > Delete account in the app, or at https://hobbycoders.com/delete-account.
