From download
to first idea.

No terminal gymnastics for the desktop installer. Bring your curiosity and connect your AI provider.

Your workspace runs on your machine. Requests to your chosen AI provider still use that provider’s services and may incur charges.

WINDOWS / QUICK START

01Install your workspace

Download the Windows installer and run it. The desktop app includes the runtimes and its own PostgreSQL database. Updating an existing installation? Keep the same installation location.

02Connect your AI provider

Open AI Shuffle and follow onboarding to configure your AI provider. Use the credentials required by that provider. Provider availability, subscriptions, and usage costs depend on your account.

03Make something your own

Create a project, choose a profile, and start a session. Describe what you want to build, then review the agent’s tool and permission requests as you go.

04Keep the ideas flowing

Return to your saved sessions, explore media tools and automations, or connect another device. The in-app documentation has detailed guides for the features you want to try next.

Before you dive in

A few things worth knowing.

Does everything run locally?

Your worker, workspace, and database can run on your computer. AI requests go to your configured provider. Media tools and connected services may also use external APIs.

Can I use a server or NAS instead?

Yes. AI Shuffle supports self-hosting with Docker so you can access your worker in a browser. Use the project’s maintained Docker setup instructions for configuration, data storage, and secure access.

Is an AI subscription included?

No provider subscription or API credit is included in this download. Configure your own supported provider and check its terms and usage costs.

Where do I get help?

Start with the in-app documentation. For questions or feedback, use the contact section on HobbyCoders. The final contact address is not connected in this design preview.
